Hugo Romat

Senior Research Engineer, Ph.D. · Microsoft Research (EPIC), Seattle

I invent and engineer new mediums for the age of AI: places where people and AI collaborate, reason, discover, and create together. I work hands-on across generative AI models, real-time interfaces, and the infrastructure that connects them, turning research ideas into working systems. I take my ideas from early prototypes to features shipped in Microsoft products like Copilot, OneNote, and Xbox. I’m passionate about building the future: making bold ideas real enough to experience, then using what we learn to shape research and products.

01

2023 — today

Media that come alive

Generative AI as an interactive, living medium.

Generative UIInteractive diffusionSpatial promptingGenerative ARVibe gaming

Why it matters

Chat made generative AI widely accessible, but a text box is only one way to work with models that generate images, video, and interfaces. I explore ways to guide these models directly through gestures, sketches, and spatial layouts, giving people more control as their ideas take shape.

Research & impact

My work explores AI beyond the chat box, from reusable prompting tools to canvases for developing ideas. It has received three Honorable Mention awards across CHI and UIST and led to eight patent filings. Alongside this research, I led work on image generation and image understanding in Copilot, and the generative image pipeline for Xbox Design Lab controllers.

Key papers

  • ImaginationVellumUIST 2025 ★ Honorable Mention

    An ideation canvas where spatial layout, generative strokes, and history become the prompt.

  • AI-InstrumentsCHI 2025 ★ Honorable Mention

    Prompts become reusable tools you apply directly to content.

  • TexterialCHI 2026 ★ Honorable Mention

    Shape text with gestures, using a language model to interpret your edits.

  • Intent TaggingCHI 2025

    Short prompts attached to specific parts of your content to guide generation.

  • Live Artifacts2026

    Dynamic media built from editable layers that retain the instructions used to generate them.

  • Brushing & Interpretive LinkingGI 2026

    Brushing and linking, reinvented for knowledge work with LLMs.

02

2019 — 2025

Ink & new input

Pen, touch, and devices that bring interaction closer to thought.

Digital inkPen + touchHandwriting AICross-deviceAccessibility

Why it matters

Writing and sketching can make it easy to capture a thought without navigating menus. Yet digital ink often remains a set of static marks on a page. I explore how systems can interpret handwriting and sketches, improve their legibility, and use them to work with data and AI, with accessibility as a design goal.

Research & impact

My research explores how ink can act on data, reshape documents, and move across devices. This work appeared at CHI, UIST, and IEEE VR and received a CHI Honorable Mention.

On Microsoft’s Pen & Ink team, I connected research with product development, leading prototypes for OneNote, Loop, and Copilot. This work led to ink straightening and sketch-to-image in OneNote, a winning Office AI hackathon, and work on an ink canvas in Loop. I also mentored Microsoft Research interns working on cross-device and pen interaction.

Key papers

  • ActiveInkCHI 2019 ★ Honorable Mention

    Ink annotations that act on the data they mark, for active reading of visualizations.

  • SpaceInkUIST 2019

    Document content reflows to make space for in-context handwritten annotations.

  • Tilt-Responsive TechniquesUIST 2020

    Digital drawing boards that adapt tools and content to how they are tilted.

  • Style BlinkCHI 2022

    Reusable handwritten styles for organizing notes and journals.

  • FlashpenIEEE VR 2021

    A high-fidelity, high-precision multi-surface pen for virtual reality.

  • AirConstellationsUIST 2021

    In-air device formations for cross-device interaction and collaboration.

  • TapTypeCHI 2022

    Ten-finger typing on everyday surfaces, decoded with Bayesian inference.

Product impact

  • OneNote · Ink straightening

    Designed and implemented the straightening algorithms that make handwriting more legible, shipped them in the OneNote codebase, and led the end-to-end evaluation through rollout.

  • OneNote · Sketch to image

    Turned our prototypes into sketch-to-image on the OneNote canvas with the Pen & Ink incubation team.

  • Loop · Ink as a first-class citizen

    1st place in the Office AI hackathon (2025): ink on the lock screen, in Loop, and in Copilot chat. The product team then started building an ink canvas into Loop.

  • Office · Sketch beautification

    Led a top-three hackathon project (2024) on beautifying sketches, picked up as a new experience across Office apps.

  • Generative ink & handwriting legibility

    Tech lead on handwriting generation, and on new ML approaches that segment ascenders, descenders, and baselines to make ink more legible and accessible.

03

2016 — 2020

Data that responds

Expressive, interactive visualization for sensemaking.

Information visualizationGraph drawingAnimationPictographsData comics

Why it matters

Interactive visualizations help people explore data, ask questions, and communicate what they find. During my Ph.D. at Inria, in collaboration with Microsoft Research, I designed systems for shaping visualizations directly rather than configuring them through menus. That idea—content you shape by hand—has guided my work ever since.

Research & impact

My Ph.D. challenged the default look of network visualizations: plain circles, lines, and static edges. I showed that expressive, animated visuals can encode more data and engage readers. This work won the INTERACT 2019 Best Doctoral Student Paper award and appeared at CHI and IEEE TVCG, and my collaborations earned a CHI Best Paper.

The idea came full circle with generative AI. I turned charts into constraints for image generation, which became the AI infographics pipeline in Microsoft Designer, and I launched a joint internship bringing data comics to Microsoft Security.

Key papers

  • GraphiesIEEE TVCG 2021

    Expressive authoring of node-link diagrams, beyond default circles and lines.

  • Animated Edge TexturesCHI 2018

    A design space for animating edges to encode data in network visualizations.

  • Particle Speed PerceptionINTERACT 2019 ★ Best Doctoral Student Paper

    How color and size change the perceived speed of animated particles.

  • Dear PictographCHI 2020

    Personalization and immersion for consuming and enjoying visualizations.

  • Sketchnote StrategiesCHI 2021 ★ Best Paper

    Components, dimensions, and strategies for effective visual note taking.

  • Interactive Data ComicsIEEE TVCG 2022

    Comics that let readers interact with the data behind the story.

Product impact

  • Microsoft Designer · AI infographics

    Developed a method that uses a chart’s depth map to guide image generation, then prototyped it with Stable Diffusion and ControlNet. The prototype led to a hackathon with the Designer team and became the SDXL pipeline for AI infographics.

  • Microsoft Security · Data comics

    Launched a joint internship with Microsoft Security that tells the story of security incidents through data comics.
